Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Who we are

TippinOut records work, tips, commission, schedules, paychecks, and other income. Published by Jennifer Quinn. Contact: support@tippinout.com.

Information you put in TippinOut

You may record workplaces and roles, clients, work hours, sales, tips, tipouts, commission, other income, paycheck amounts, schedules, notes, and optional cashout photos. Your appearance, calendar, notification, and backup settings are also saved. You decide what to enter and whether to attach a photo.

Your records and local storage

TippinOut stores these records and settings on your device to show history, calculate summaries, create reports, schedule local reminders, and make backups. The app does not require an account or upload your work and income database to a TippinOut server simply because you enter information. Cashout photos are stored as app-private files referenced by the local database.

Rolling local safety snapshots stay within app storage. These snapshots can be lost if the app is removed or the device is lost.

Usage and crash monitoring

Firebase-enabled versions use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app use, measure reliability, and fix problems. These services send monitoring information off your device to Google. Monitoring is separate from your locally stored records.

Monitoring can include app/version details, device model and operating system, app installation identifiers, launches and sessions, usage events, and crash diagnostics. Analytics can derive approximate location, such as country or region, from network information. TippinOut does not request GPS location for monitoring.

We do not intentionally attach income amounts, notes, employer or client names, or cashout photos to monitoring events or crash reports. Diagnostics can contain technical error messages and application state. Installation identifiers distinguish installations; monitoring should not be treated as completely anonymous.

Advertising identifier collection and advertising-related Analytics settings are disabled in this version. TippinOut does not display ads or use this monitoring for advertising personalization. Monitoring operates automatically where collection is enabled; the app currently has no separate monitoring opt-out switch. Turning off reminders does not turn off monitoring.

Google processes monitoring information under its service terms, privacy practices, and retention settings. Processing may occur outside your country. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups, exports, and other services

When you make a portable backup or export, TippinOut hands the file to your device's save/share options. You choose where it goes. Android can make automatic portable backups to an external folder you select. Backups may include records, settings, clock sessions, and cashout photos. PDF/CSV exports include information shown in their preview.

Files saved to a cloud drive, email app, or another destination are handled by that service under its practices. Saving a backup outside TippinOut keeps a restorable copy if the app or device is lost.

Device access and reminders

Notification permission is requested when you enable scheduled-shift reminders or set a clock-out reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally. Notification Settings controls scheduled-shift reminders; individual clock-out reminders are managed on the shift clock. Phone notification permission controls delivery of both. For photos and backup restores, the system picker lets you select the file used by TippinOut.

Your choices, deletion, and contact

Edit/delete entries in Calendar and manage workplaces and roles in Jobs. Restoring a portable backup replaces local records after confirmation. Removing the app may remove local data and snapshots. Copies saved elsewhere remain until you delete them there. Deleting local records or uninstalling does not necessarily delete monitoring information already received by Google.

For privacy questions or help with a deletion request, contact support@tippinout.com. We do not have your local database to retrieve or delete remotely. Identifying a particular installation's monitoring may require additional information because the app has no account. If you email support, your email address/message are used to respond and handled by the email services involved.

Changes

We will update this notice if data handling changes, including if accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or subscriptions are introduced.
